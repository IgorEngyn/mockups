What Does the Process for B2B Generative Engine Optimization Services Look Like?

B2B GEO work should follow the route from qualification through stakeholder evaluation to the sales handoff. Sales and technical owners supply approved capabilities, limitations, and proof that can be published. Each review point should confirm both factual accuracy and usefulness to the relevant buyer role.

We map each accepted correction to the buyer question it resolves and the owner who can approve it, preserving the distinction between a supported capability and a separately scoped customization.

  • Sample questions asked by evaluators, budget owners, and procurement teams.
  • Identify unsupported claims or missing explanations in the cited source pages.
  • Publish approved improvements that help buyers qualify fit and prepare a useful sales conversation.
  • Retest the selected questions and report factual progress separately from pipeline observations.

How Does a B2B AEO Agency Track AI Answers and Brand Mentions?

A B2B AEO agency should organize its question set by buying role and evaluation stage, including the evidence procurement needs to check. Log each answer, citation, date, and factual discrepancy so repeated observations remain comparable across a long sales cycle. Brand mentions are one signal within that sample and cannot by themselves establish influence on a closed deal.

We compare like-for-like question groups over time and record material changes in the offer, so a broader procurement sample does not distort a trend based on earlier technical questions.
