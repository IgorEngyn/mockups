What Is Included in Google Ads Services in the US?

Google Ads services organize campaigns around search intent, relevant keyword groups and a clear conversion goal. Work includes ad copy, bidding configuration, landing-page alignment and measurement checks, followed by agreed testing and maintenance. The proposal should state whether it covers search campaigns alone or other campaign types, and whether page changes are recommendations or implementation work.

We map each promoted service to a suitable destination and a meaningful conversion action. Combining unrelated services on one generic page can make it difficult to see which offer produced a useful inquiry.

What Should You Agree on Before Outsourcing Google Ads Management?

Before delegating Google Ads management, settle account ownership, billing and campaign approval rights. Provide the services you want to promote, geographic exclusions and any claims that require internal review. Document who maintains conversion tags and landing pages, and make the transition plan explicit so an agency change does not leave campaigns without an accountable manager.

We request access through the account's supported permission controls and agree an approval owner for sensitive claims. Billing and administration should remain understandable to your business throughout the engagement.

How Do You Compare Google Ads Management Packages?

Compare Google Ads packages at the campaign level: which services are advertised, which campaign types are managed and how new tests enter the schedule. Check whether the agency writes ads, maintains exclusions and investigates tracking problems, or only advises your team. Landing-page development and CRM implementation deserve separate scope lines rather than being assumed from the phrase full management.

Our scope explains how many distinct offers need campaign preparation and what happens when one changes. A new service launch may require new messaging, exclusions and a different qualification path.

What Does the Process for Google Ads Services Look Like?

Google Ads delivery starts with the existing account and ends each cycle with a decision about the next test. Your team supplies service priorities and approves claims before ads go live.

We document the intended customer action before checking measurement. This lets us test whether a booking, request or purchase reaches the correct destination and is recorded once.

  • Review account history, conversion records and the landing pages available.
  • Group search intent into campaigns and prepare ads, exclusions and bidding settings.
  • Test the enquiry path and conversion actions, then obtain launch approval.
  • Examine search terms and sales feedback to refine the next campaign iteration.

What Affects the Cost of Hiring a Google Ads Agency?

Our Texas-based team's paid ads management starts at $750/month, or 10–15% of ad spend for larger budgets; the applicable fee depends on scope. The cost of Google Ads support changes with the breadth of the account and the condition of its measurement. Multiple service lines, new landing pages and offline lead reporting require more setup than a focused search campaign with working tracking. Google's media charges remain separate from agency work, as do any external tools selected for the account.

We flag dependencies such as an unfinished landing page or missing sales records during scoping. Resolving them can change the initial workload even when campaign coverage stays the same.

What Should Be Reviewed Before Increasing Google Ads Spend?

Before increasing spend, confirm that tracked conversions represent leads your business wants. Review search terms, geographic relevance, conversion coverage and the outcome of recent inquiries, then check whether sales staff can handle additional demand. If tracking is duplicated or the offer attracts unsuitable prospects, raising the budget can magnify the problem instead of producing useful growth.

We also examine whether recent leads have had time to progress through your sales process. Comparing immature leads with older closed business can make a sound campaign look weaker than it is.

How Can Google Ads Conversion Tracking Support Bidding Decisions?

Conversion tracking supports bidding when the actions selected for optimization reflect business value. Google Ads can use directly measured conversions or conversions imported from Google Analytics; check their counting settings and avoid counting the same action twice. Verify which goals each campaign uses for bidding, test the events and reserve observation-only actions for reporting when they do not represent the outcome you want to buy.

We distinguish a request being submitted from the business accepting it as qualified. Where the necessary systems and consent arrangements support it, that later outcome can inform a more useful measurement plan.
