What Does the Process for Marketing Attribution Services Look Like?

Attribution work starts by defining the business question and the records available to answer it. Your team supplies system access and agrees on the meaning of a qualified lead or sale.

We document how a lead becomes an opportunity or sale before joining records. Matching a form submission to the wrong later outcome can produce a convincing but misleading report.

  • Map channels, events and CRM stages to the outcomes the report must explain.
  • Test source capture and reconcile sample records across connected systems.
  • Select a credit-assignment approach whose assumptions fit the available data.
  • Review the findings with decision-makers and document blind spots before using them for allocation.

What Can Attribution Reports Explain About Incremental Growth?

An attribution report describes how recorded outcomes are credited, not how many would disappear without the marketing activity. A customer may have purchased anyway, and several channels may claim involvement in the same journey. Controlled experiments, such as an appropriately designed holdout, can help estimate incremental impact when feasible; a change in attributed revenue alone does not prove causation.

We label the assumptions behind attributed results and keep causal questions separate. Where an experiment is feasible, we agree its comparison groups and decision criteria before looking at the outcome.
