What Is Included in Car Dealership Digital Marketing Services in the US?

Dealership marketing connects available vehicles with buyers who want more information or a test drive. Research and channel planning guide inventory content, approved offers and campaign creative. Initial work should define vehicle inquiry and appointment measurement, with ongoing feed management and CRM integration included only when specified.

We keep the vehicle identifier with the campaign and its destination. This makes it possible to update or withdraw a specific vehicle without confusing it with similar inventory.

What Does the Process for Car Dealership Digital Marketing Services Look Like?

Dealership campaigns start with current inventory and approved offer terms. Sales staff confirm the inquiry and test-drive process before assets go live. Review then follows vehicle-specific interest into actual appointments.

We confirm that the advertised vehicle details match the current stock record at publication. Sales should also know which offer prompted the inquiry.

  • Research buyer needs for the inventory the dealership wants to promote.
  • Select channels and prepare vehicle assets and offers for approval.
  • Check listing accuracy and CRM routing before campaign launch.
  • Use test-drive outcomes and availability feedback to adjust the campaign.

How Does a Car Dealership Marketing Agency Connect Campaigns with Sales Follow-Up?

A dealership inquiry should carry the vehicle identifier and requested next step to a named sales contact. The team agrees on a response expectation and confirms availability before arranging a test drive. Marketing receives appointment outcomes and common objections, allowing it to improve inventory messaging without equating every inquiry with a vehicle sale.

We separate test-drive requested, confirmed and attended in reporting. Those stages reveal whether interest is being lost during contact, availability confirmation or the visit itself.
