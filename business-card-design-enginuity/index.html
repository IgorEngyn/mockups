What Does the Process for Professional Business Card Design Services Look Like?

Professional business card design starts by verifying contact details and brand assets. The designer develops layout options, checks the chosen approach against print dimensions and finishing requirements, then incorporates consolidated feedback. The final proof needs explicit approval of both the information and artwork before production files are released.

We separate factual approval from visual approval so a correct layout does not hide an incorrect phone number or title. Both checks refer to the same final proof.

What Should Be Verified Before Business Cards Are Printed?

Before printing, verify every name, role and contact detail against the approved business information. Check the proof at actual size, confirm printer specifications and test any QR code using the final artwork. Make sure the logo, fonts and images are cleared for the intended use, then record approval of the exact file being sent to production.

We test any QR code from the production proof and confirm the destination is appropriate for the recipient. A working code is only useful if the next page serves the intended purpose.

How Should Card Materials and Print Specifications Influence Design?

Card materials and finishes should influence the design before the artwork is finalized. Dimensions determine the available space, while texture, coatings or foil can affect contrast and the treatment of fine detail. Use the printer's requirements for bleed, safe areas and finishing layers, and check that the chosen typography stays readable at the actual printed size.

We review fine text and small marks against the selected finish. Decorative treatments should not make essential contact information harder to read.
