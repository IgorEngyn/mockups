What Is Included in Google Local Services Ads Management in the US?

Google Local Services Ads management begins with eligibility and profile readiness, then covers service selection, operational coverage and lead handling. The agreed scope should identify who supplies verification information, updates the profile and reviews incoming inquiries. Advertising management cannot replace Google's approval process or the business's responsibility to provide accurate credentials.

We check the service list against jobs your staff can accept today. Profile readiness includes accurate business information and a practical response plan, alongside whatever verification Google currently requires.

What Does the Process for Google Local Services Ads Management Look Like?

An LSA project needs a usable business profile and a working response process before lead volume becomes meaningful. The stages are:

Our readiness review includes a sample lead handoff: who receives it, where it is recorded and who follows up if the first person is unavailable.

  • Check Google's current availability and verification requirements for the business category and location.
  • Gather business information and configure services, hours and genuine coverage areas.
  • Assign the people who answer and record incoming leads; approve budget settings.
  • Review lead records, booked jobs and profile accuracy as advertising continues.

Launch readiness depends on both platform approval and the team's ability to respond.

How Should Google LSA Management Handle Service Areas and Incoming Leads?

LSA service areas should reflect where your team can actually accept work, with services and operating hours kept accurate. Assign a primary responder, a backup and a process for recording qualification and follow-up. Eligibility and verification depend on Google's current category and location requirements; management should check those requirements before presenting a business as ready to advertise.

We review coverage by job type as well as geography. A team may serve an area for planned installation while lacking capacity to offer urgent repairs there.

What Affects the Cost of Google Local Services Ads Management?

Our Texas-based team's paid ads management starts at $750/month, or 10–15% of ad spend for larger budgets; the applicable fee depends on scope. LSA costs have two distinct parts: Google's charges for valid leads and the work needed to manage the account. Lead prices vary with factors including location, job type and bidding settings, while management effort depends on profile preparation, service coverage and reporting. Additional call-handling or CRM work belongs in the project scope.

We separate initial profile preparation from ongoing lead review and reporting. Existing verification records and a working intake process can materially change the work required.

How Can Local Services Ads Lead Quality Be Assessed?

Assess LSA lead quality by recording service fit, contact outcome and booked work for each inquiry. Separate unsuitable requests, missed opportunities and customers who proceed so the team can identify whether coverage, response handling or demand is the issue. Google's lead assessment and available feedback or credit mechanisms do not guarantee a refund for every unbooked lead, so reconcile billing status separately from sales results.

We compare the reason a lead did not book with the service advertised. Repeated requests for excluded work suggest a profile or coverage issue; unanswered valid inquiries point to a response problem.
