What Information Should Supplement Stores Verify Before Publishing Website Content?

Qualified reviewers should check ingredient evidence, stated limitations and both explicit and implied health claims. The FTC's health-products guidance explains the need for truthful, substantiated marketing. Ranking is not evidence of a health effect, and a study about an ingredient may not support the claim made for a particular product.

We ask your reviewer to check the exact formulation and serving context behind a proposed claim, rather than treating a reference to one ingredient as approval for the entire product description.
