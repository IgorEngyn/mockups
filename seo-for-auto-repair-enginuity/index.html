A symptom search and a repair booking need different answers.

Repair pages should help a driver identify a relevant shop and understand the assessment process. Search visibility cannot make an unsupported vehicle or repair claim accurate.

Service pages that hide vehicle coverage

What Information Should Auto Repair Shops Verify Before Publishing Website Content?

Shop management and a qualified technical reviewer should verify vehicle coverage, service descriptions and documented credentials. Check that the site does not imply support for a system or repair the shop cannot handle. High visibility can amplify an inaccurate claim, creating unsuitable appointments and expectations before an adviser speaks to the customer.
