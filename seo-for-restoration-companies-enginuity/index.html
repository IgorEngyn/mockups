What Information Should Restoration Companies Verify Before Publishing Website Content?

Restoration management should verify service scope, staffing availability and the evidence behind credentials. Claims about response or project outcomes need to match what the team can substantiate. Search prominence cannot guarantee restoration results, and exaggerated availability may mislead a property owner seeking timely assistance.

We ask your team to confirm whether a credential applies to the company, a named person or a particular service, and keep the published wording within that documented scope.
