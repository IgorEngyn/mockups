What Does the Process for Auto Repair Digital Marketing Services Look Like?

Auto repair campaigns need an accurate service offer before they need an advertising schedule. The shop supplies supported vehicles, available appointments and approved repair information. After the booking path is tested, performance reviews should examine repair fit and appointment outcomes.

We check the booking handoff using a realistic vehicle inquiry. The service adviser needs the advertised offer and requested work, not just an unexplained phone number.

  • Research driver demand for the repair services the shop wants to prioritize.
  • Choose channels and create service assets for the shop's review.
  • Test phone and form handoffs to advisers, then launch.
  • Use bookings, declined requests and estimate feedback to improve the campaign.

How Does an Auto Repair Marketing Agency Connect Campaigns with Sales Follow-Up?

Auto repair inquiries should give service advisers the vehicle details and requested work needed to start a useful conversation. The adviser owns follow-up and confirms an appointment or the need for further assessment within the shop's agreed response process. Reporting should return booking status and reasons for non-fit without treating an online inquiry as a confirmed diagnosis or repair sale.

We record appointments separately from repairs authorized after assessment. This avoids reporting an initial inquiry as revenue before the shop knows what work is appropriate.
