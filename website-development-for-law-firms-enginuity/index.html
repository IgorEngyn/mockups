What Questions Can Law Firm Website Design Consultants Help You Answer?

Consultants can help decide which intake information belongs on the public site, how requests reach the appropriate practice team, and when document submission needs a separate secure channel. Bring the firm's current intake procedure, assignment rules, and approved service descriptions. The assessment should produce page and workflow decisions with owners and unresolved dependencies clearly identified.

We would trace a representative inquiry through the current process before recommending fields or connections. That reveals which information belongs on the website and which should be requested later.

What Does Full-Service Law Firm Website Design Include from Start to Finish?

A full-service law firm website project connects the agreed content, design, development, intake routing, and launch work under one delivery plan. Attorneys approve legal statements, the firm's intake owner confirms request handling, and the implementation team verifies the approved connections. Handover should identify who maintains pages and forms afterward; confidential document workflows or case-system features require explicit inclusion.

We keep the final published wording tied to the firm's approval. Changes made during design or shortening need another check when they alter the meaning of a legal statement.
