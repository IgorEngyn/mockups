What Information Should Manufacturers Verify Before Publishing Website Content?

Engineering and commercial reviewers should verify materials, tolerances, capabilities and procurement requirements. Published specifications must reflect the conditions under which the company can meet them. Search visibility is not capability verification, and an overstated tolerance or unsupported process can lead a buyer to request unsuitable work.

Before we publish a tolerance or performance statement, engineering should confirm its applicable material, process and measurement conditions rather than approve the number in isolation.
