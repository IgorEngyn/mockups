What Is Included in AI SEO Audit Services in the US?

An AI SEO audit establishes what selected answer systems currently say about a business and where those answers lack reliable support. Deliverables should include an approved question set, dated answer samples, a citation review, and a prioritized list of factual or content gaps. Implementation and future retesting should be explicitly included or left as separate follow-on work.

We attach each issue to the exact observed answer and a verified business reference, making the deliverable usable for correction rather than leaving your team with an unexplained visibility score.

What Does the Process for AEO Audit Services Look Like?

An AEO audit moves from an agreed sample to a reviewed action plan. Your team supplies the service facts and identifies who can confirm whether an answer is accurate. Each stage should have a clear completion point.

We freeze the agreed question list for the baseline, then record any later additions separately so the final comparison does not silently change what was tested.

  • Approve questions that represent the audience and buying decisions being studied.
  • Collect dated answers and their cited pages under recorded test conditions.
  • Compare the claims with verified business information and document factual gaps.
  • Rank findings by customer impact and the ability to address their source.
  • Review the evidence and assign owners to the accepted recommendations.

What Evidence Should a Generative Engine Optimization Audit Record?

A generative engine optimization audit should retain the exact prompt, observation date, platform, available configuration details, answer text, and cited page addresses. Each factual issue needs a comparison with the approved business information and a record of whether it recurred. A single response is evidence of one observation, not proof that every prospective customer receives that answer.

We preserve enough context to distinguish a brand mention from a linked citation and an accurate statement from a misleading one; those are separate observations with different implications.

Audit observationEvidence we retain
Business mentionThe exact wording and recorded test conditions
Linked citationThe cited page and the claim it is used to support
Factual discrepancyThe conflicting statement and approved business reference

Which Questions Should an AI Visibility Audit Test?

Test questions that match how the intended audience discovers, evaluates, and contacts a provider. Include service-fit questions, meaningful buying constraints, and branded factual checks, with the selection method recorded before testing begins. Avoid choosing only prompts likely to mention the company, and label the results as findings from that defined sample.

We include neutral discovery questions alongside branded fact checks, giving the audit a way to examine both whether the business appears and whether existing descriptions are correct.

How Can AI Audit Findings Become a Prioritized Content Plan?

Turn audit findings into content tasks by identifying the missing or inaccurate fact, the page that should support it, and the person authorized to approve the correction. Prioritize errors that could mislead a customer before expanding less consequential topics. Retest the affected question groups after publication, recognizing that improved source material does not guarantee an immediate change in generated answers.

We assign the correction to the page that should own the fact and identify any reused wording, so fixing one source does not leave another public page contradicting it.
