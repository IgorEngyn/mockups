What Should You Agree on Before Outsourcing SEO Services for Financial Services Businesses?

Outsourced financial SEO needs defined page, technical and reporting deliverables. Your business retains responsibility for financial claims and onboarding decisions, with controlled website access and designated review points. Specify continuing support and any form changes; confidential financial documents should stay within the client-service process rather than become SEO working material.

For our engagement, agree which reviewer can authorize a correction and how urgent changes reach the published pages rather than remaining in an approved document.
