What Information Should Affiliate Websites Verify Before Publishing Website Content?

Affiliate editors should verify current specifications, the basis of first-hand claims and clear disclosure of commercial relationships. A comparison must distinguish observed experience from information supplied by a manufacturer. Visibility is not evidence of product testing, and inaccurate recommendations can distort a reader's purchase decision.

We retain the basis and date of a comparison so your editor can reassess it when a model changes, a specification is corrected or the commercial offer no longer matches the page.
