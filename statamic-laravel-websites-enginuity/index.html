How Does a Statamic Development Agency Plan and Deliver a Project?

A Statamic project begins by mapping the content your team publishes and the relationships between entries. Your editors review those structures and sample screens before the templates and any Laravel integrations are completed. Launch follows content and functional testing, with approval from both the publishing and technical owners.

We suggest approving a small set of representative entries before migrating the full library, including long copy, missing optional fields and relationships between content types.

  • Inventory content and agree Collections, fields and editorial responsibilities.
  • Design the site using representative entries and review the editing workflow.
  • Build templates and integrations, then test publishing and visitor actions.
  • Approve migration and launch, with deployment and maintenance documented.

How Should a Statamic Development Agency Structure Content for Editors?

Structure Statamic content around the entries editors actually manage, with Collections grouping related items and blueprints defining their fields. For example, a project entry can have a title, images and related services without requiring an editor to rebuild its page layout. Reuse shared content where appropriate and assign permissions so publishing responsibility stays clear as the site grows.

Our advice is to distinguish fields editors must complete from optional material that the template can omit cleanly. Shared field definitions help keep repeated information consistent, while instructions beside an unfamiliar field can prevent avoidable publishing mistakes.

What Should Editors Receive When a Statamic Website Launches?

Editors should receive accounts with the permissions needed for their work and instructions based on the site's actual content structures. The handover should demonstrate creating an entry, reviewing it and publishing or updating it through the agreed workflow. Name the person responsible for technical maintenance, so editors know where to take a problem they cannot resolve through content changes.

In a launch review with us, an editor should complete one real update using their own assigned permissions. That exercise checks whether the instructions, available controls and approval process match the responsibilities the person will actually have.
