How Should an E-commerce SEO Agency Report on Organic Traffic and Leads?

E-commerce SEO reports should distinguish organic product browsing from completed transactions. Assess performance by relevant categories and include cancellations or returns when the data supports it. Recorded revenue is not profit, and multichannel journeys can prevent a complete attribution of each sale to search.

We compare organic orders with product availability and, where accessible, returns data, helping distinguish a search-discovery problem from stock shortages or product expectations that lead to cancellations.

What Information Should E-commerce Businesses Verify Before Publishing Website Content?

Merchandising and customer-service teams should verify product specifications, availability, fulfillment conditions and return terms. Information must describe the item customers will actually receive. Search placement cannot correct inaccurate stock or delivery promises, and those mistakes can turn a successful visit into a disputed order.
