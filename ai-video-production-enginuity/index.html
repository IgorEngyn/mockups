What Does the Process for AI Video Creation Services Look Like?

AI video work begins with a concept and a decision about which scenes can use generated material responsibly. The team develops and selects assets, edits them into a coherent sequence and reviews accuracy, continuity and brand representation. Your approval should cover both the creative direction and the final export, with unsuitable generated details corrected or replaced before delivery.

We approve the scene's purpose before generating variations, then judge candidates against that purpose. Attractive imagery still needs to support the intended explanation.

How Should Generated Video Assets Be Checked Before Publication?

Check generated video for factual accuracy and misleading visual details before publishing. Review product appearance, text, actions and continuity, then verify permissions and usage conditions for source assets and any recognizable likenesses. Do not let a synthetic scene imply that an event, endorsement or customer result actually occurred when it did not; the final human review must consider the message as well as visual quality.

We check what a reasonable viewer would infer from the scene. Generated footage must not turn an illustrative idea into apparent evidence of a real event or result.

What Should an AI Video Project Specify About Revisions and Ownership?

An AI video agreement should define final exports, revision boundaries and the editable assets that can be handed over. Record usage conditions for generated and third-party material, together with responsibility for approving likenesses and factual claims. Because tools and source assets can have different terms, clarify ownership and permitted use for the actual production setup instead of assuming every element has identical conditions.

We document the usable deliverables and the conditions attached to their component assets. This is more reliable than treating an entire AI-assisted project as having one uniform rights position.
