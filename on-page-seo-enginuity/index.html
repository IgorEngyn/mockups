When Should Existing On-Site Content Be Revised or Consolidated?

Revise a page when its purpose remains useful but its information, structure or intent coverage is weak. Consider consolidation when several pages serve the same need without a meaningful distinction, after checking their traffic, links and business role. Low traffic alone does not justify removal; preserve genuinely different services and plan URL handling if consolidation changes where content lives.

We suggest comparing the actual inquiries each overlapping page supports before combining them. Two pages with similar keywords can still serve different buyers or stages of a project and deserve separate treatment.
