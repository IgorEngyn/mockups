What Information Should News and Media Publishers Verify Before Publishing Website Content?

Editors should verify sources, author identities, publication dates and the handling of corrections. Updates must make substantive changes clear rather than merely refreshing a date. Search prominence does not establish accuracy, and an unsupported statement can mislead readers regardless of how easily they find it.
