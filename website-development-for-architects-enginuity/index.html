What Does the Process for Architecture Web Design Services Look Like?

The process begins with a portfolio inventory, image rights, project credits, and the kinds of briefs the firm wants to receive. Approved content and design directions move into development, including the agreed consultation request route. Testing checks image performance, mobile reading, and brief delivery before launch, followed by an editing handover for future projects.

We establish which projects are built, in progress or represented through visualizations. That distinction stays visible through content approval and final presentation.

How Can Website Design for Architecture Firms Make Customer Inquiries Easier to Handle?

An architecture inquiry form can ask for project type, location, stage, and the prospective client's main objectives. Passing that context into the consultation workflow helps the responsible architect decide whether a discovery conversation is appropriate. Provide clear ownership and a fallback contact option if the form or CRM connection fails, without making an inquiry appear to reserve design capacity.

We ask for an initial brief that helps the firm judge fit without turning contact into a full design questionnaire. Location, project type and stage usually inform the first review.

What Affects the Cost of Website Design for Architecture Firms?

For a Webflow build, our Texas-based team's pricing runs from $1,500 for a landing page to $25,000+ for a custom website, depending on scope. Other platforms require an individual quote. Architecture website costs are shaped by portfolio depth, image preparation, bespoke layouts, and the requirements of the project-brief workflow. Copywriting, private areas, or ongoing portfolio updates add scope; external photography, hosting, and licensed tools should be costed independently.

We assess image preparation and project storytelling before estimating bespoke layouts. A portfolio with strong source material can need a different scope from one requiring substantial editorial assembly.
