What Is Included in ChatGPT SEO Services in the US?

ChatGPT SEO work can include reviewing public content for factual clarity, checking search-crawler access, and monitoring a defined set of answers. OpenAI's crawler documentation distinguishes OAI-SearchBot access for search from GPTBot settings for potential model training. Agree on the pages and access checks to be completed, with ongoing answer monitoring specified separately; access alone does not guarantee citation.

We review search access separately from training preferences and check the live page response as well as the declared crawler setting, because permission alone does not establish that useful content can be retrieved.

What Does the Process for ChatGPT SEO Services Look Like?

The process starts with the questions your customers ask and ends with documented changes and a repeatable observation method. Your team confirms business facts and approves any access or publishing changes. Completion means the agreed improvements have been verified, rather than a promised placement in an answer.

We record the published correction and its verification before repeating the sample, keeping our completed website work distinct from any later change in ChatGPT's answer.

  • Select customer questions and record a baseline of sampled ChatGPT search answers.
  • Review cited pages, factual gaps, and any relevant crawler-access restrictions.
  • Approve and publish precise improvements to the supporting website content.
  • Check the live pages and repeat the agreed questions with dates and conditions recorded.

What Can ChatGPT Search Reports Show Reliably?

A ChatGPT search report can reliably document what was observed for the recorded questions on the stated dates. It can show citations, brand mentions, factual errors, and differences between repeated samples. It cannot establish a permanent ranking or guarantee that another user will receive the same answer.

We report a mention, a citation and a referral visit as different events; observing one does not establish that the others occurred or that an inquiry resulted.

Reported eventWhat the evidence establishes
Mention in a saved answerThe business appeared in that recorded response
Citation to a pageThat response included the documented source reference
Recorded referral visitThe website observed an attributable visit within its measurement limits
