How Does a Window Company Digital Marketing Agency Connect Campaigns with Sales Follow-Up?

Window campaign inquiries should reach a sales owner with the property location and requested replacement or installation work. The owner agrees on the next response and arranges measurement or product discussion as appropriate. Reporting can return estimate status and reasons for non-fit, helping marketing avoid promoting options the company cannot supply or install.
