What Does the Process for Landscaping Company Digital Marketing Services Look Like?

Landscaping campaign development follows the work you want on the calendar. You supply service coverage, project examples and seasonal capacity, then approve the proposed offers. Site visits and suitable quote requests determine whether the launched campaigns need adjustment.

Before promoting a seasonal offer, we confirm the work and capacity it represents. Project photos should show your contribution and match the service being offered.

  • Research demand by property type and maintenance or installation need.
  • Choose channels and prepare approved project visuals and request forms.
  • Check that inquiries reach the estimating team and launch the campaign.
  • Review site-visit results, quote fit and maintenance interest before reallocating effort.

How Does a Landscaping Marketing Agency Connect Campaigns with Sales Follow-Up?

Campaigns should pass the property location, requested landscaping work and preferred contact details to the estimator. That person owns the response and decides whether a site visit is appropriate within an agreed follow-up window. Feedback on project fit, accepted quotes and recurring-maintenance interest helps marketing distinguish valuable demand from unsuitable volume.

We separate maintenance interest from requests for site visits and larger estimates. This lets reporting reflect the different follow-up and scheduling decisions each inquiry requires.
