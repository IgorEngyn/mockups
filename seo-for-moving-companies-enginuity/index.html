What Information Should Moving Companies Verify Before Publishing Website Content?

Estimating and operations staff should verify move-size limits, origins, destinations and actual service scope. Availability claims need to reflect current scheduling capacity. A searchable page is not a reservation, and inaccurate route or date information can lead customers to expect a move the company cannot accommodate.
