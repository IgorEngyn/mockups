How Do You Measure Traffic and Leads from SEO Services for Landscapers?

Landscaping reports should separate organic interest in maintenance from demand for larger design or installation work. Track relevant page visits and quote requests, then connect them with site-visit and project-fit feedback. Seasonal shifts and later referrals complicate attribution, so a traffic increase alone does not establish how many landscaping contracts search produced.

We recommend grouping inquiries by maintenance, design and installation before evaluating growth; a shift toward unsuitable one-off requests can be hidden inside a rising lead total.
